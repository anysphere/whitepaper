Programming Jokes Collection
============================

Here's a collection of programming jokes to brighten your day while working on secure communication systems!

---

**Joke #1: The Debugging Expert**

A programmer walks into a bar and orders 1 beer, 2 beers, 3 beers, 4 beers, 5 beers...

The bartender says, "You seem to be stuck in a loop."

The programmer replies, "Don't worry, I'll break out of it eventually."

---

**Joke #2: The Optimistic Developer**

Why do programmers always mix up Halloween and Christmas?

Because Oct 31 equals Dec 25!

(For those who don't get it: 31 in octal = 25 in decimal)

---

**Joke #3: The Security-Conscious Coder**

What's a programmer's favorite type of music?

Algo-rhythms!

And their favorite security measure?

Multi-factor authentication... because one factor is never enough!

---

**Joke #4: The Database Administrator**

SQL walks into a NoSQL bar, but can't find a table.

---

**Joke #5: The Version Control Expert**

Why did the git commit go to therapy?

Because it had too many issues to push!

---

Remember: In the world of secure communication, laughter is the best encryption for your mood! 😄

(But seriously, use proper encryption for your messages - that's what Anysphere is for!)
